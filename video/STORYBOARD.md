# Five-minute IBM Bobathon video

**Strict duration ≤ 5:00.** Recommended editing target **4:45–4:55**. Based on the official Video-Instruction PDF, the video must cover all **four dimensions** below.

| Time | Required dimension | Content | Suggested speaker |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:10 | **1 — Challenge & Learning** | Parkinson ON/OFF, debiased OFF objective, grouped patient visits and the core learning challenge | Member A |
| 1:10–2:20 | **2 — IBM Bob Usage** | Show actual Bob skills, EDA, skore reports and a real moment Bob helped solve a problem | Member B |
| 2:20–3:30 | **3 — Technical Continuity** | Describe setup, reusable lessons, role of GitHub/skore and how team practices could continue in future ML projects | Member C |
| 3:30–4:45 | **4 — Overall Experience** | Team organization, work distribution, challenges, support received, things to improve, main takeaways | Member D |
| 4:45–4:55 | Closing | One evidence-based final takeaway | Everyone / captain |

### Capture list — actual evidence only
- [ ] Team introduction and participant permission to show their image.
- [ ] Screenshot of Bob using one legitimate lab skill.
- [ ] EDA graph with no raw competition rows shown.
- [ ] A Skore report with correctly labelled RMSE and evaluation protocol.
- [ ] GitHub project structure and documented teammate collaboration.
- [ ] Confirmed Kaggle rank/scores, only if officially available.
- [ ] A real difficulty and a real solution — do not fabricate a success narrative.

### Quality rules
1. Speak naturally; avoid reading an uninterrupted wall of text.
2. Keep plots large enough to read on a phone.
3. Check spelling of **MDS-UPDRS**, **RMSE**, **GroupKFold**, **Skore**, **Bob**.
4. Use consistent sound levels, clear captions and accessible contrast.
5. Verify the export duration **does not exceed 5:00**.
6. Watch the entire final cut with all four members before uploading.
7. Do not assert a result, rank, skill usage or permission that is not supported by actual evidence.

The four official dimensions contain multiple example questions. Address them concisely across the narrative rather than reading every question verbatim.
